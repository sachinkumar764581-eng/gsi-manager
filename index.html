Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Coaching Institute Manager. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Institute name, owner name, email address, and phone number to create your account.
  • Student Data: Student names, contact details, and fee payment histories entered by you.
  • Images: Institute logo and student photos processed locally and safely.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for the core functionality of the app (managing students and generating receipts). We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertising or marketing agencies.

4. Data Storage & Safety

All data is stored securely in your device's Local Storage to allow offline usage. A backup of this data is securely synced to a centralized Google Drive repository for auto-restoration.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data. You can delete your local data instantly by using the "Delete Account & Data" option in the App Settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, you may email our support team via the Help & Support section.
